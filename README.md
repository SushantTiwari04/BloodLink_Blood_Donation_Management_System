# 🩸 Blood Link – Blood Donation Management System

**Blood Link** is a blood donation management platform designed to connect blood donors with people in need of blood. It makes the process of finding donors, registering as a donor, and requesting blood easier, faster, and more accessible.

The platform allows users to register themselves as blood donors, search for donors based on their blood group, and locate nearby donors based on their location. Users can also submit blood requests for both emergency and normal situations, helping people connect with potential donors when they need them most.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Become a Donor:** Register yourself as a blood donor and help save lives.
- **Find Blood Donors:** Search for donors based on specific blood groups.
- **Emergency Blood Requests:** Submit urgent blood requests to find donors quickly.
- **Normal Blood Requests:** Create regular blood requests for planned medical needs.
- **Nearby Donor Search:** Discover potential blood donors near your current location.
- **Donor Connectivity:** Connect people who need blood with people willing to donate.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of Blood Link is to make blood donation more accessible by reducing the difficulty of finding suitable donors. It aims to build a connected community where people can help one another during medical emergencies and contribute to saving lives.
